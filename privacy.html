FF Sensitivity VIP: Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil

1. Bu ilova haqida

"FF Sensitivity VIP" (muallif: ASIDRJ) o'yinchilarga telefon modeliga qarab sensitivity (sezgirlik) va boshqaruv tugmalari joylashuvi (HUD) sozlamalarini ko'rsatadigan ma'lumotnoma ilovasi.

2. Biz qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

Ilova sizdan hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot so'ramaydi va yig'maydi. Ro'yxatdan o'tish, akkaunt, ism, telefon raqami, joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera yoki mikrofon ishlatilmaydi.

3. Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma'lumot

  • Tanlagan tilingiz telefoningizning o'zida saqlanadi, bizga yuborilmaydi.
  • Ilovaning tez ochilishi uchun sahifa nusxasi qurilmada vaqtincha saqlanadi (kesh).

4. Texnik ma'lumotlar

Ilova internet orqali ishlaydi. Ilova joylashgan hosting xizmati (Cloudflare) har bir so'rov uchun IP manzil kabi standart texnik jurnal ma'lumotlarini xavfsizlik va barqarorlik maqsadida qayd etishi mumkin. Biz bu ma'lumotlarni shaxsni aniqlash yoki profil yaratish uchun ishlatmaymiz.

5. Reklama va uchinchi tomonlar

Hozirgi versiyada ilova reklama ko'rsatmaydi va tahlil (analytics) vositalaridan foydalanmaydi. Kelajakda reklama (masalan, Google AdMob) qo'shilsa, bu siyosat yangilanadi va qaysi ma'lumotlar ishlatilishi shu yerda aytiladi.

6. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va ulardan ataylab ma'lumot yig'maydi.

7. Bog'liq emasligi haqida

Bu ilova rasmiy emas, fan-made loyiha. U Garena yoki Free Fire o'yini egalari bilan bog'liq emas va ular tomonidan tasdiqlanmagan. Free Fire va boshqa nomlar tegishli egalariga tegishli.

FF Sensitivity VIP: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. About this app

"FF Sensitivity VIP" (author: ASIDRJ) is a reference app that shows players sensitivity settings and control button layouts (HUD) for different phone models.

2. What data we collect

The app does not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, account, name, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Your selected language is stored on your own device only and is not sent to us.
  • A copy of the page may be cached on the device so the app opens faster.

4. Technical data

The app works over the internet. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may record standard technical log data such as IP addresses for security and reliability. We do not use this data to identify you or build profiles.

5. Ads and third parties

The current version shows no ads and uses no analytics tools. If ads (for example Google AdMob) are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the data involved.
